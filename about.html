About

I’m Nathan Maldonado — a writer, researcher, and technologist interested in language, memory, archives, and digital storytelling. My work lives somewhere between computation and culture.

Background

I recently graduated from Duke University, where I studied Computer Science, Linguistics, and Psychology. My interests include sociolinguistics, queer archives, digital humanities, and computational approaches to language.

Across research, design, and writing, I’m interested in how identity becomes legible through language — particularly in relation to race, class, migration, and sexuality.

Outside of academics, I document fragments of everyday life: cities at night, family photographs, overheard conversations, music, transit systems, and internet ephemera.
