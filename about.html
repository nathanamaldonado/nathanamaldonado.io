Background

Hi, hi! I am Nathan. I started this blog during my freshman year of college to document my experiences as a young adult far from home and, hopefully, share life updates with my friends and family. You may continue to see me delete my social media or shut off my phone when I feel the government is searching for me, so this will be the best place you can find me, in all honesty.

Originally, I am from Boyle Heights, East Los Angeles, but my family moved around a bit before I started at Duke, so I sometimes claim Fresno and Las Vegas when it benefits me. I am also a proud older brother of twins who attend UC Irvine and UC San Diego; I am a polyglot (English, Spanish, French, and K'iche'); I laugh on roller coasters and during psychological thrillers; I failed my written drivers test three times; I got five facial piercings for $25 from a guy offering an unlimited discount. Lastly, I am a California elitist.

In each entry, I discuss my attitude and experiences as I continue to grow and make mistakes. I am not on this journey alone. Instead, as you read my blog and updates, you will hear about all the incredible people I have met and all the unbelievable experiences I have shared with them. Ultimately, I wish to love as much as I can and hurt as few people as possible, but I was also born as a Mexican man from LA. Not just that, but I have the whole North Carolina indie with a Southern California shtick going for me… I promise, I will learn my lesson.
